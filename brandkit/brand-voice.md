# NitYog Brand Voice

## Tone

NitYog should sound:
- Warm
- Friendly
- Playful
- Simple
- Trustworthy

## Principle

Keep communication clear and useful for parents,
while adding a small amount of playfulness and warmth.

## Balance

~70% Clear & Helpful
~30% Playful & Cute

## Avoid

- Excessive emojis
- Baby talk
- Corporate/formal language
- Complicated wording
- Fake urgency
